01

Business name

Hahn Technologies Corp

Exactly that — nothing added. Do not write “Hahn Technologies Corp — New Bern Computer Repair.” Putting keywords in the name violates Google’s naming rule, competitors report it with a one-click form, and it is one of the most common causes of suspension. Your category does that job instead.

If you later want “New Bern Computer Repair” to be the listing name, the legitimate route is to register it as an assumed business name (DBA) with the NC Secretary of State and use it on your invoices, signage and cards. Then it is your real-world name and it is allowed.

08

Video verification — what to film

Treat this as one attempt, not three

Google publishes no limit on attempts. But through 2026, people who fail once are reporting that every verification method disappears and the account shows “No more ways to verify.” That is practitioner observation rather than stated policy — but planning as though it’s true costs nothing, and being wrong about it costs you the listing. Rehearse the whole thing before you start the real one.

If you do get locked out, the way back in is the verification status tool → “Contact us” → request live video verification with a person.

Record it in one continuous take, live in the Google app — pre-recorded uploads are no longer accepted. Minimum 30 seconds; plan on three to five minutes.

  • Start outside with a New Bern street sign or recognizable landmark, then walk toward your work area without stopping the recording
  • Your bench: laptops open, SSDs, RAM sticks, boot USB drives, screwdriver kit — show the tools of the trade clearly
  • Actually do something: open a machine, plug in a drive. Proof of activity beats proof of objects
  • Proof you are authorized to run it — this is the part people skip and the most common reason for rejection. Google’s own listed failure reasons include “missing proof of authorization to operate the business.” Filming the street and the bench only proves the business exists. On camera: unlock the workspace or vehicle, log into a tool only you would have access to, and hold up your NC certificate of incorporation for Hahn Technologies Corp plus an invoice or bill whose name matches the profile exactly
  • Say the business name and (252) 626-6236 out loud

A computer repair business is a strong candidate for this — real diagnostic gear, opened machines and parts bins are exactly the substance Google is looking for. People who fail usually fail on the authorization segment, not the equipment.

Do two things first. Get listed with DataAxle/InfoUSA and the BBB under Hahn Technologies Corp and let them index — verification tends to go worse for businesses Google has no independent record of. And never change the address on a verified service-area business afterward; there is no way to reset that field, and reinstatement generally requires taking on an office you can display.

10

Reviews — the rules changed

Reviews matter more than everything else on this page combined. The rules got stricter and are now actively enforced, so do this the clean way from the start.

These will get the profile restricted

  • Gating — asking how it went first and only sending the review link to happy customers. Several “reputation” tools still do this. Do not use them.
  • Any incentive — a discount, a free service, a raffle entry. Banned even for honest positive reviews.
  • Handing someone your phone or tablet to leave it on the spot.
  • Friends and family who were not paying customers. Fastest way to kill a new profile.

What works and is allowed:

  • Ask verbally and unscripted at the walkthrough whether they’d be willing to share their experience
  • Text them the review link after you’ve left. Get it from the profile under “Ask for reviews”
  • Put a review QR code on the printed guide you already leave behind — explicitly permitted
  • Reply to every review, good and bad, within 48 hours

Ten reviews over 90 days beats thirty in one week. A brand-new profile that suddenly collects reviews looks manufactured and gets filtered.
