Before you use this: it reflects what the website already promises, so the two match. It is not legal advice and nobody who wrote it is a lawyer. Item 5 (the limitation of liability) is the one worth having a North Carolina attorney glance at — a clause drafted too aggressively can be struck out entirely, which leaves you with nothing. Bring this draft to that appointment so you are not paying someone to start from a blank page.

Why the owner’s name is asked for separately in section 1: under NC law a repairer’s lien on unclaimed property is capped at $100 when you dealt with someone other than the owner. A great deal of your work comes in through an adult child dropping off a parent’s laptop, so that one extra line is worth filling in.
